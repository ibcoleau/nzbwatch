SABnzbd status reconciliation

When SABnzbd automatically clears completed downloads and history, NZBWatch may be unable to verify the final outcome.

Default: 60 minutes, starting when the job first goes missing.
Assumed complete is not verified success. Deleted, failed or manually removed jobs could also disappear. No confirmed-completion Pushover notification is sent for assumptions.

Notifications

Automatic retention

NZBWatch prunes aged records automatically, usually within an hour. Zero disables a cleanup rule.

Default 14. Deletes old completed/error/skipped/dismissed results and event log entries. Active queued and unreviewed found matches are protected.
Default 7. Starts from the last successful or assumed-complete download, after all downloads are finished. Failed/incomplete watches stay.
Cleanup deletes local NZBWatch records, not SABnzbd downloads. NZBWatch retains its grab fingerprint ledger to prevent duplicate auto-downloads even after cleanup. Back up /data before changing retention.
Secrets are stored in /data/config.json (mode 0600). Keep backups private.

Watchlist backup

Import/export watch rules only. Connection secrets are excluded; back up your Unraid appdata separately for full recovery.
