How it works

The scheduler runs inside the container. Each enabled watch searches NZBHydra2 at its configured interval, applies title, keyword, category, size and age filters, and remembers matches in SQLite. Automatic watches send the NZB to SABnzbd and track completion; notify-only watches leave the queue decision to you.

Tip: Use specific search terms and filters. For one-off searches, keep “Maximum grabs” at 1. Set it to 0 only when you deliberately want multiple matching releases to be downloaded over time.
